  <p class="entry-period">Approval gateway</p>
  <div class="prose">
    <p>Nothing happens until someone says yes.</p>
    <p>
      Gatekeeper holds a risky action until the right person approves it. A
      deploy, a payment, a database migration, an agent's next move. Once it's
      approved, Gatekeeper keeps a signed record of who said yes, when, and why.
    </p>
    <p>

      approve from your phone.
