# ninetynine.systems

[ninetynine.systems](https://ninetynine.systems) is my software studio. Most of
what I build there is about AI agents doing real work, and the plumbing you need
before you can trust them with it.

## Gatekeeper

Nothing happens until someone says yes.

Gatekeeper holds a risky action until the right person approves it. A deploy, a
payment, a database migration, an agent's next move. Once it's approved,
Gatekeeper keeps a signed record of who said yes, when, and why.

It's written in Rust, on axum and SQLite, with a `gk` CLI, a hash-chained audit
ledger, a React dashboard, and an Expo app so you can approve from your phone.

[gatekeeper.now](https://gatekeeper.now)
